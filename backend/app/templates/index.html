How HeritageRisk AI Works

1

Detect

Citizens upload guided smartphone photos of heritage sites. Notes describe visible damage — cracks, corrosion, graffiti, water staining, vegetation growth.

2

Analyse

AI or manual review identifies visible risk indicators. A rule-based risk score is calculated. The system creates a structured risk case with an evidence report.

3

Verify

A human reviewer verifies the case, updates the status, and confirms the damage assessment before any action is taken.

4

Route

Verified cases are routed to the responsible site owner, local council, heritage authority, or conservation team. Response and action status is tracked.
