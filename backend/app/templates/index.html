Human-in-the-Loop Workflow

1

Submit

A contributor uploads site details, notes, and one or more safely taken photos.

2

Gate

A human checks privacy, sensitivity, relevance, and evidence quality before AI access.

3

Analyze

Azure OpenAI or the mock fallback drafts visible indicators, uncertainty, and a next step.

4

Finalize

The reviewer accepts, edits, or rejects the AI draft and confirms final tags and severity.

5

Track

A Risk Case and evidence report are created, then manually verified, routed, and closed.
